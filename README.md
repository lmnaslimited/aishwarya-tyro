A food-delivery company uses LENS for taking their food orders. The kitchen flow works smoothly, and Delivery Notes are created in the LENS. The operational problems start only when the food is ready for delivery. 
 
Drivers depend on printed paper slips of Delivery Note to know which order to take. This leads to repeated problems: 
    • Slips get misplaced, torn, or lost. 
    • No one can confirm who picked an order first. 
    • No clear record of when a driver left for delivery. 
    • When customers ask for an update, the team cannot give an immediate or accurate answer. 
 
To find the status of an order, staff must search through WhatsApp messages, call drivers one by one, and sort through unorganized paper slips. This creates delays, customer complaints, and internal frustration. Arguments between drivers and staff are frequent because there is no reliable tracking.
 
The company also provides delivery incentives. Orders under 2 km delivered within 30 minutes earn extra points. But since drivers record the times themselves, the data is inconsistent. Some report correctly, some forget, some guess, and some adjust times later. Weekly incentive reports rarely match reality, causing conflict between kitchen staff, drivers, and management. 
 
The company wants to improve this part of the workflow without replacing LENS. They want a simple Nextjs interface that drivers can use on their phones so delivery activities are tracked clearly and the confusions are solved.
